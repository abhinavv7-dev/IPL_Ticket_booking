Matchday Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Ensure you have your digital E-ticket or printed ticket with a clear QR code ready for scanning at the entry turnstiles.

ID Proof Requirement

A valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID) matching the booking name is mandatory for entry.

Prohibited Items

Bags, Outside Food & Beverages, Cameras, Power Banks, Coins, and Lighters are strictly prohibited inside the stadium grounds.
